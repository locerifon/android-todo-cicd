TodoApp is a modern, reliable, and fast task management application engineered following the highest standards of mobile development and post-release maintenance.

Key Features:
• Intuitive task tracking: manage, organize, and prioritize daily activities with ease.
• Dynamic Feature Toggles: experience instant feature activation powered by Firebase Remote Config without waiting for store updates.
• High Stability: continuous monitoring via Google Play Android Vitals and Crashlytics.
• Privacy & Security: zero unnecessary permissions, fully encrypted transport.

Developed as part of post-release deployment & maintenance coursework.
